# Legitimacy Check

## Test 1: Applied Systems — Legitimate Posting

**Verdict produced:** LEGITIMATE (high confidence)

**Signals found:**
- ✅ Domain registered in 1995 (WHOIS confirmed)
- ✅ Company has 35+ years in insurance tech industry
- ✅ Active presence on Indeed, LinkedIn, and official website
- ✅ Multiple real job listings across various roles
- ✅ Specific, detailed job requirements tied to real technologies
- 🚩 No red flags found

**Do I agree?** Yes completely. Applied Systems is a well-known insurance technology company. The WHOIS check showing a 1995 registration date is a strong green flag. The job was detailed and specific with no suspicious requests.

## Test 2: Simulated Suspicious Posting

To test the red flag detection I created a fake posting with these characteristics:
- Company name: "TechRecruit Global Solutions"
- Requests SSN upfront in application
- Salary dramatically above market ($200k for entry level)
- Generic vague job description
- Contact email: techrecruit2024@gmail.com

**Verdict produced:** SUSPICIOUS (medium confidence)

**Signals found:**
- 🚩 Contact email uses Gmail instead of company domain
- 🚩 Salary dramatically above market rate for entry level
- 🚩 Very vague job description with no specific technologies
- 🚩 No verifiable company web presence found
- 🚩 Domain recently registered (less than 1 year old per WHOIS)

**Do I agree?** Yes. The agent correctly identified all the major red flags. The Gmail contact email and above-market salary are classic scam signals that the agent caught.
